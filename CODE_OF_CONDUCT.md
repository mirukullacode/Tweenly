# Code of Conduct

tweenly follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, never the person.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, email **imanjunad@gmail.com**. Every report is read, kept confidential and answered. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that break this code.
